This Majesty perfume ad was an opportunity to explore luxury through visuals and storytelling. I wanted to give the fragrance a royal, sophisticated feel, bringing together cinematic shots, detailed product visuals, dramatic lighting and a rich atmosphere.

The entire film was conceptualised and created using AI tools, experimenting with how far AI filmmaking can go in creating premium brand commercials. From the smallest product details to the overall visual composition, the focus was on making the film feel as close to a high-end luxury advertisement as possible.
