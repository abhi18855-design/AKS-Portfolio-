## Finding the story in the stillness

This is a **demonstration project**, ready to be replaced with your own film. The landscape is an original vector placeholder, not a film still. The optional player embeds *Big Buck Bunny*, an openly licensed Blender Foundation film, solely to demonstrate video playback. It is not portfolio work by the site owner.

Use this space for your creative concept, the story behind it, and the choices that shaped the final piece.

## The process

Tell the story in your own way. Add as much or as little as you need: a location, an unexpected detail, or a note on your role. Upload your frames below and let the work do the rest.
