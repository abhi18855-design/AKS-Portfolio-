An AI-powered brand film for PREET Ethnic Wear, celebrating the richness of India's diverse textile traditions. Featuring the timeless elegance of Banarasi and Kanjivaram sarees, the film brings together two iconic weaving cultures through festive aesthetics, intricate craftsmanship, and cinematic AI-generated visuals. It captures the beauty of heritage passed down through generations while presenting traditional Indian fashion through a contemporary lens.
